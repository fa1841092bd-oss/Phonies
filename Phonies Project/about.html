About Our Store

Discover our journey in the world of premium timepieces and our commitment to quality and style.

Our Story

Founded with a passion for precision and craftsmanship, Watches has become the premier destination for luxury timepieces in Abbottabad and beyond.

From Humble Beginnings to Timeless Elegance

Watches was founded in 2015 in the heart of Havelian District, Abbottabad, with a simple mission: to provide our customers with premium quality timepieces that combine style, functionality, and affordability.

What started as a small watch repair shop has evolved into one of the region's most trusted watch retailers, offering an extensive collection of luxury, sports, and smart watches from renowned brands worldwide.

Our journey has been driven by a deep appreciation for horological craftsmanship and a commitment to helping our customers find the perfect timepiece that reflects their unique style and personality.

Today, we continue to serve our community with the same passion and dedication that has defined us since day one, while constantly expanding our collection to meet the evolving needs of watch enthusiasts.
